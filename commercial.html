FAQ

Common questions

How much does commercial cleaning cost?

It depends on the number of restrooms, break rooms and desks, your floor types and how often we clean. After the walkthrough you get the scope in writing.

Do you clean after hours?

You set the days and times around your shifts, deliveries and visitors.

Who will be cleaning our building?

Our own in-house crew. We never use subcontractors, and the owner checks the work on site.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We can issue a certificate naming your company as certificate holder on request.

Do you clean warehouse floors?

No. We focus on offices, break rooms and restrooms inside warehouses. Production floors that need ride-on scrubbers are outside our scope.
