03 What’s included

The same checklist, every visit.

Our regular weekly or bi-weekly service that keeps your home consistently clean.

Every room

  • Dust all reachable surfaces, furniture, shelves and décor
  • Dust ceiling fans and light fixtures (within reach)
  • Wipe baseboards (spot, within reach)
  • Wipe light switches, door handles and doors (spot)
  • Remove cobwebs
  • Clean mirrors and glass, streak-free
  • Empty trash and replace liners
  • General tidying and straightening
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Sweep, vacuum and mop all hard floors

Kitchen

  • Wipe and sanitize countertops and backsplash
  • Clean and shine sink and faucet
  • Wipe exterior of all appliances
  • Clean stovetop surface
  • Clean inside and outside of microwave
  • Wipe exterior of cabinets (spot)
  • Wipe table and chairs
  • Sweep and mop floor

Bathrooms

  • Clean and disinfect toilet: inside, outside, base and behind
  • Scrub and disinfect shower / tub
  • Clean and shine sink, faucet and countertop
  • Clean and polish mirror
  • Wipe tile within reach
  • Fold and straighten towels
  • Empty trash
  • Sweep and mop floor

Bedrooms & living

  • Make beds / change linens (fresh linens if provided)
  • Dust surfaces, nightstands, dressers and décor
  • Dust lampshades and ceiling fan blades (within reach)
  • Wipe window sills
  • Tidy and straighten
  • Vacuum floors and carpet

Everything in Standard, plus the detailed, built-up areas a regular clean doesn’t reach. Recommended for first-time clients, seasonal resets, or homes that haven’t been professionally cleaned recently.

Whole home

  • Hand-wipe baseboards, full detail
  • Detailed dusting of window sills
  • Wipe doors, door frames and trim (full)
  • Detailed dusting of décor, picture frames and knick-knacks
  • Clean light switches and outlet covers
  • Detailed vacuuming of edges, corners and under reachable furniture

Kitchen

  • Deep clean inside the microwave
  • Hand-wipe cabinet fronts (full)
  • Deep, degreased backsplash
  • Detail around and behind small appliances
  • Clean and shine stovetop with degreaser
  • Wipe down range hood exterior

Bathrooms

  • Deep scrub of grout and tile
  • Remove soap scum and hard-water buildup
  • Descale showerhead and fixtures
  • Detail around the base of the toilet and fixtures
  • Hand-wipe cabinet fronts and vanity

Available on request with any Standard or Deep Cleaning. Just ask when you book.

08 FAQ

Common questions.

Still unsure? Call (973) 391-6392

How much does a cleaning cost?

Every home is a different size with different needs, so there’s no one-size price. Call us at (973) 391-6392 with your ZIP code, bedrooms, bathrooms and the type of cleaning, and we give you a price, with or without products.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most of our clients aren’t home while we clean. We’ll arrange access with you when you book.

Is it the same crew every time?

Yes. The same crew comes at the same time, every visit.

Are your cleaners background-checked?

Yes. Every cleaner is part of our own team, employed by Elite Prime Clean: trained and background-checked. We never send subcontractors, franchise teams or app workers.

What if something isn’t right?

Before the crew leaves, we do a quality walkthrough and ask for an OK on the work. Anything that needs attention is fixed right there.

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Your choice. We quote with or without products. Family- and pet-safe products, with an eco-friendly upgrade available.

What’s the difference between Standard and Deep cleaning?

Standard keeps your home consistently clean every week or two. Deep cleaning includes everything in Standard plus detailed work like full baseboards, grout, soap scum and degreasing. We recommend it for your first visit.

Do you clean Airbnbs and vacation rentals?

Yes. We do turnover cleaning between check-out and check-in for hosts and property managers. One call to set up your properties.

Do you speak Spanish or Portuguese?

Yes. We speak English, Spanish and Portuguese.

How can I pay?

Cash, Venmo, Zelle, credit card or debit card.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel up to 48 hours before your service at no charge. Later cancellations are charged.

Do you also clean offices?

Yes. Commercial cleaning is a big part of what we do, including Red Bull facilities and Barrymore Film Center. See commercial cleaning.
