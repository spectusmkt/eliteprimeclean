Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Elite Prime Clean (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit eliteprimeclean.com, fill out a form, call or text us, and how we use it. We are a cleaning company based in Cliffside Park, New Jersey.

Information you give us

When you use a form on our website, we collect what you type in, which may include:

  • Your name, phone number and, if you choose to share them, your email address and building address
  • Your company name (for commercial requests)
  • Your ZIP code and details about the cleaning you need, such as type of space or home, bedrooms, bathrooms, how often, key dates and any notes

When you call or text us, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us in the call or message.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ours records some information about your visit:

  • Campaign details. If you arrive from an ad or a tracked link, we save the campaign tags in that link (for example UTM tags or ad click IDs) together with your form, so we know which ads bring customers.
  • Analytics. We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how people use the site, such as pages viewed, the device and browser used, approximate location (city level) and clicks on our call and text buttons. These tools use cookies and similar technologies. We do not send your name, phone or email to Google Analytics.
  • Advertising. We use Google Ads and the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) to measure our ads and to show ads to people who visited our site. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to record that a visit, a tap on our phone or text buttons, or a form request came from one of our ads. We do not send your name, phone or email to Google Ads or Meta.

How we use your information

  • To answer your call or message and give you a price
  • To schedule walkthroughs and cleanings, and to stay in touch about your service
  • To understand which pages and ads work, to show our ads to people who visited our site, and to improve our website
  • To keep basic business records and meet legal obligations

We do not sell your name, phone number, email or the details of your request. Information about your visit (such as pages viewed and button taps) is shared with Google and Meta through the advertising tools described above. Some state laws treat this as “sharing” for targeted advertising; see “Your choices” to opt out.

Who we share it with

We use a few service providers to run our business, and they handle information only on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Workspace / Google Sheets and Gmail) to store form requests and notify us of new ones
  • Google (Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads) for website analytics and ad measurement
  • Meta (Meta Pixel) for ad measurement and to show our ads on Facebook and Instagram
  • GitHub, which hosts this website
  • Our phone and messaging carriers, to make and receive calls and texts

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Text messages

If you text us, we will reply to your messages about your cleaning request. We do not add you to marketing text lists. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Reply STOP at any time and we will stop texting you.

How long we keep it

We keep requests and customer records for as long as we need them to provide service and keep business records, and then delete them.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what information we have about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by calling or emailing us.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • To limit ads based on your visits, use Google My Ad Center, your Facebook ad preferences, or the industry opt-out at optout.aboutads.info.
  • Depending on the state you live in, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us and we will respond as the law requires.

Children

Our website and services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
